FAQ

Does she read my code?
The token ledger uses local usage records, including token counts and model names. It doesn’t need your source files. If you turn on “Read your files” for cLAudE, she can also read files in Desktop, Documents and Downloads. The Privacy Notice lists the data each feature uses.
Who pays when I chat with her?
You do, through your own accounts. cLAudE uses the claude CLI on your computer with your Claude subscription, CodeX uses codex with your ChatGPT subscription, BlueGiantFish uses the DeepSeek key you enter. The app never touches your logins or passwords.
Will browsing and drawing on her own burn through my quota?
Those are off by default. Once on, she uses small, cheap models for her own time (Haiku, gpt-6-luna), and you decide how often she goes out. Settings → Usage shows what the app itself has spent.
What does the leaderboard upload?
The leaderboard is off by default. Turn it on to share your display name and cumulative usage. You can also add a region and a project. The public entry shows when it was created and last updated. The Privacy Notice lists all upload fields and explains which ones are public.
What happened to the trees?
The previous edition was the one that grew trees. The trees and the map are gone, replaced by AI characters in the corner of your screen. The leaderboard is the same one and your score carries over.
Why cLAudE and CodeX? Are you connected to Anthropic or OpenAI?
No. They're fan characters, with the capitals scrambled on purpose. Claude, Codex and the other names are trademarks of their owners.
Can I change who sits on the desk?
Yes, any time in Settings. The other three (KiMi, ZeeFox, GemiNyan) are still being finished and only drop by when you use Kimi, GLM or Gemini.
macOS says it can't verify the developer
The app isn't signed with a paid Apple certificate. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. You only need to do it once.
Does the house cost real money?
No. Homes, food and books are bought with the tokens you've spent, which is a number the app keeps, not money.
Will she keep interrupting me?
No. She doesn't use system notifications. When she wants you, a small card of ours appears in the top-right corner; hover to keep it, click × to dismiss. You can turn it off in Settings.
Where is my data, and can I delete it?
On macOS in ~/Library/Application Support/TokenForest/, on Windows in %APPDATA%\TokenForest\. Her memory and chat history can be wiped in Settings; you can ask for your leaderboard row to be deleted.
Does it cost anything?
It's free for now.
