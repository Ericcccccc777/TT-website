8. WeChat Link and third-party services

8.1 WeClaw. The WeChat Link relies on WeClaw (https://github.com/fastclaw-ai/weclaw), an open-source program licensed under the MIT License by its own authors. You install WeClaw yourself; we do not bundle, modify, distribute or support it, and we give no warranty for it. The Software only starts and stops WeClaw, shows its sign-in QR code, edits the specific entries of WeClaw's configuration file described in the Privacy Notice, and relays the messages WeClaw hands it for the AIs handed to the Software (Claude, Codex, DeepSeek), as described below. The Software shows WeClaw's official install command for you to copy; it never runs it.

8.2 Tencent. WeClaw uses Tencent's WeChat ClawBot (iLink) interface. Your use of WeChat and ClawBot is governed by Tencent's terms. Messages pass through Tencent's servers, and Tencent may rate-limit, block or end the service at any time. You are responsible for your WeChat account.

8.3 AI providers. Messages are answered through the Claude Code CLI (Anthropic), the Codex CLI (OpenAI) or the DeepSeek API, using your own accounts and keys. The Software adds the answering character's persona prompt to each message and, together with the recent conversation WeClaw passes along, hands it to that character's AI: for Claude and Codex, it runs the claude or codex CLI that you installed and signed in to yourself, without your own settings and without tools unless you turn some on (below), and never handles your Claude or ChatGPT sign-in or tokens; for DeepSeek, it sends it to the DeepSeek API with the key you saved. It returns the reply to WeClaw. Use through the Claude Code CLI and the Codex CLI counts against your own plan limits. With "Remember our chats" on (the default), it also keeps that conversation on your computer and once a day has that character's AI summarize it into short notes the character uses later, as described in the Privacy Notice; you can view, turn off or clear this in Settings. If you turn on "Let her text first" (off by default), the Software may, within the limits described in the Privacy Notice, ask the character's AI whether she wants to message you and have WeClaw send that message to the WeChat account you signed in with; it only ever messages that account. If you turn on "Her own time" (off by default), the Software may, within the limits described in the Privacy Notice, read Wikipedia, Hacker News and other public web pages on the character's behalf from your computer, give what it reads to the character's AI, and keep short notes about it; it does not post, sign in or act on any website. If you turn on any of the "What she can do" switches (all off by default), the Claude and Codex characters may, within the limits described in the Privacy Notice, search the web and open links you sent, read and write files in their own folder, read files in your Desktop, Documents and Downloads folders (Claude only), send files to your WeChat through WeClaw, draw pictures by running your own codex CLI (using your ChatGPT plan), and look at pictures you send; what they read or see is sent to Anthropic or OpenAI as part of the conversation. You decide what to switch on and are responsible for the files you let them read and send. Notes written by an AI from your conversations, messages she starts, files she makes or sends and pictures she draws may be incomplete, wrong or unsuitable. Their terms and usage policies apply, including their rules on supported countries and regions. Claude and ChatGPT are not available in mainland China or Hong Kong; do not use the WeChat Link to reach a service from a place where its provider does not offer it. You are responsible for what you send and receive, and for any charges those providers make.

8.4 Availability. The WeChat Link is not available on Windows. It may stop working if WeClaw, WeChat or the AI providers change.
