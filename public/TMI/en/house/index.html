Come over to their place

The tokens you spend are their money. Save enough for a home and they move in, in 3D, and cook, read, feed the fish and play games on their own. Look down on the whole place, or press V to walk in.

Three homes

54 m²120 m²270 m²

Studio

54 m²

One room and a lounge, with a balcony. Games on the TV, a dartboard on the wall.

Tokens to move in
10M tokens
The balcony table

A little round table sits beside the railing, just outside the lounge. Look along the balcony and you can see the washing hanging up.

The mint-green kitchen

The fridge and sink share one wall. The little round table beside them has room for dinner.

Big one-bedroom

120 m²

Two round rooms joined together: a pool room, a games room, a bedroom, and a motorised screen hidden in the ceiling.

Tokens to move in
100M tokens
The card table

Three chairs circle the red table. Just enough for a game of Dou Dizhu.

The window seat

The sofa follows the curve of the windows. Take a seat and invite her to watch a video you’ve added.

Full-floor flat

270 m²

A tatami room just for tea, everyone on the floor; a glass greenhouse along the west side with birds, a parrot, koi and a tortoise; a bowling lane, an arcade and a claw machine.

Tokens to move in
1B tokens
Tea on the tatami

Everyone takes a spot on the floor around the low tea table.

A turn at the arcade

Pick a fighter in the flat’s Compute Arena cabinet. Play head to head, best of three.

Screenshots are from builds in progress; the rooms are still changing.

What they do at home

Everyday life runs in the page itself and doesn't touch your quota: she picks something to do by the clock, walks over, does it, then thinks of the next thing. The better the home, the more there is to do.

What you do for real, she does at home

When your AI is working, she walks to the computer; when a WeChat message comes in, she stops where she is and types with both hands. Browsing, her diary and drawing are her own time: click her then and she won't answer.

Fifteen mini-games

Hands wobble in darts and balls rebound off the pool cushions. Rules and physics run locally. In Who’s the Spy, a model takes a turn at judging.

Every home

And also

Watch together

Sit on the sofa and pull her in to watch a video you've added. She sees the title and subtitles, and the picture only if you turn on "Let her see the screen".

Music

The record player plays the built-in public-domain tracks, and can control Apple Music and Soda Music.

Things to buy

Food, tea, books, newspapers. When something runs out she'll mention it, gently.

Their voices

Lines in cards and mini-games are pre-recorded. The app doesn't connect to any speech service.
