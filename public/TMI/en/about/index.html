About & credits

From a tree to a desk full of company

It started as a tree: the tokens you spent coding with AI grew into a pixel tree on your desktop.

Then we noticed people wanted something that talks back. In October 2026 the trees and the map went, and AI characters moved into the corner of the screen: they keep the books, give you a nudge, chat with you, and live on your tokens.

The leaderboard didn't change. It's the same board, and old scores carry over.
