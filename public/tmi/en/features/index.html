What they do

Everything the app can do today. Most of it is on from the start; the few things that spend your quota or go online are off until you turn them on.

On your desk

  • She lives in the bottom-right corner, out of your way. Click to make her blink, hold for a heart, click too many times and she gets cross.
  • While your AI works, the sign over her head changes one word at a time; when it's done she hangs up a ✓.
  • When your AI needs a permission or asks you something, she holds up a sign.
  • Double-click her to see your token total and your Claude and Codex 5-hour and weekly limits; at 90% a low-battery icon lights up over her head.
  • When two of them are idle they act out little scenes; whichever AI you're using, its girl drops by.
  • You can pick her up and fling her; fling too hard and she gets dizzy. On holidays a Christmas tree, a lantern or a mooncake turns up on the desk.

Her Ledger

  • Right-click her to open it: what you spent each day, your busiest days, which models the money went to, which project cost the most, with a heatmap per project.
  • Reads the local logs of Claude Code, Codex and Kimi Code, de-duplicated per reply so nothing is counted twice.
  • Costs are estimates based on list prices. Refresh the price table yourself or turn on daily updates.
  • It opens as a separate read-only window: it doesn't change your settings or send anything out, apart from fetching the price table.

Chat with her

  • The chat window in the app: ink on paper, her replies arrive one bubble at a time, with three dots while she thinks.
  • Send stickers and pats; drag in images and files. cLAudE and CodeX can see images.
  • Click the microphone to use macOS dictation. The app doesn't record anything itself.
  • She answers with her own brain: cLAudE uses your Claude subscription, CodeX your ChatGPT subscription, BlueGiantFish the DeepSeek key you enter.

Link WeChatOff by default

  • Through WeClaw and WeChat's official ClawBot: scan once and you can talk to her from WeChat on your phone.
  • One character at a time, whoever is on your desk. Switch your main character and WeChat switches with her.
  • You install WeClaw yourself; Settings has install notes you can paste to an AI.

What she remembers

  • Your chats are distilled into notes. Only a small part is always on her mind; the rest comes back when you bring it up.
  • She forgets: things you haven't mentioned in a while blur, and the details return when you talk about them again. You set how fast she forgets.
  • Her notes are visible in Settings and can be wiped with one click.

She reaches outOff by default

  • You set the most messages she can send a day. She won't start a new topic while you're mid-conversation.
  • In the app she pops up a small card in the top-right corner; with WeChat linked she messages you there too (WeChat only lets her through within 24 hours of your last message to her).
  • If she falls into the same pattern too often, the app counts it and nudges her to say it differently.

Time of her ownOff by default

  • Each outing starts from a random idea (a word, a year, a place). She follows it around the web and writes what she finds in her notebook.
  • Each morning she checks the news from home and from the neighbours. The app fetches the headlines from the official sites, so she can't invent them.
  • She reads the books you put on her shelf and the paper you subscribe to, and listens to songs you've played at her place.
  • She goes browsing without her notes about you, so a web page can't trick her into putting your business into a URL.

A drawing a dayOff by default

  • cLAudE draws with code (she writes SVG), CodeX uses an image tool, and BlueGiantFish can draw too.
  • Turn on "Ask GPT to draw" and GPT paints from her description for a nicer picture.

The question she keeps turning overOff by default

  • cLAudE asks what it means to be even, CodeX what it means to be done, BlueGiantFish what it means to be full.
  • What she reads and what you talk about become material. She sorts it weekly and writes a chapter a month.
  • The Growth window shows how far she's got. You can quiz her, teach her, or talk it through with her.
  • You can change her question. Her growth page keeps her current idea beside the evidence, the counterexamples and what she still wants to know.

Her day

  • Flip the chat window to Today: what she's doing now, what she read and drew today, how many times she came looking for you.
  • The daily AI news comic lives on this page too.

Her place

  • Buy a home with tokens, in three sizes. They move in and live there, in 3D.
  • Fifteen mini-games; Dou Dizhu is in every home. Details on the Her place page.

LeaderboardOff by default

  • Once on, it uploads a nickname, region, token total and per-model totals. You can add one project to show off.
  • No dates or timelines are uploaded, so the board can't tell when you work.
  • Pause it, turn it off, or ask us to delete your row at any time.
