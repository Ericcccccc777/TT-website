Her memory palace

What she remembers isn't a list. In Settings it's an ∞ with 0s and 1s flowing through it; open it and another window shows eighty thousand characters wound into one stream. You can turn it, zoom in, and click a line to read it in full.

Hover over the ∞ to see which kind of memory each strand is.

  • Long-termKept for good: about you, people you care about, your running jokes, what she has said she thinks
  • Mid-termA short summary for each day you talked, kept for the last 30 such days
  • Short-termWhat's on her mind these days and what's still pending; rewritten every day
  • Talking with youWhat she has learned from how you react; she follows it when replying
  • What comes up often sticks; what doesn't blurs, and the details come back when you talk about it again. You set how fast she forgets.
  • Forgetting isn't deleting: old things that get pushed out go into a ledger nobody sees, and she only digs into it when there's a strong clue.
  • All of it stays on your computer. The website and the leaderboard can't see a single word.
