Talk to her

The app has a chat window, and you can link WeChat too. She thinks with your own accounts: cLAudE uses your Claude subscription, CodeX your ChatGPT subscription, BlueGiantFish the DeepSeek key you enter.

  • She remembers what you've talked about, and slowly forgets: what comes up often sticks, what doesn't fades.
  • She'll reach out on her own. You decide how many times a day, at most.
  • They have their own sticker sets, and you can pat them.
